    How this works

    The model never draws. It returns a spec: a title, a summary and three to six layers, each with a label, a sentence of explanation, a colour, a shape and a share of the stack. The page validates every field — colours must be hex, shapes come from a fixed list, every number is clamped — and then builds the SVG itself. Pull the slider apart to see the inside; click a layer to read it.

    Each drawing spends your own Pollen through Bring your own Pollen. Your key goes nowhere except gen.pollinations.ai and stays in this tab's session storage. The price printed under the diagram comes from the usage the API reports and the per-token prices in /models.

    Tested models are marked ✓ in the picker; ⚠ means the model was not measured on this prompt and may answer with prose instead of the object.
